LingoIQ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Welcome to LingoIQ! We take the privacy of your personal data very seriously and are committed to maintaining transparency regarding how we collect, use, and protect your information.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you visit our website or use our language-learning platform, as well as how we process that data.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

We only collect information that is necessary to ensure full platform functionality and to save your learning progress:

  • Account Data: When you register or log in via Firebase Authentication, we receive your email address, username, and selected avatar.
  • Learning & Progress Data: We store information about the languages you are learning, your vocabulary (glossary), rules, training stages, learned word statistics, and review dates. All of this data is securely stored in a Firebase Firestore database.
  • Technical Data & Cookies: We may use local storage (localStorage) and sessions solely to maintain your authentication state and interface preferences (such as your current interface language or selected mode).

2. How Do We Use Your Data?

The collected information is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your account within LingoIQ.
  • Synchronizing and saving your personal progress, glossaries, and rules across different sessions.
  • Providing technical support and enhancing user experience on the platform.

3. Data Protection and Security

  • We use industry-leading security infrastructure solutions provided by Google Firebase to safeguard your data during transmission and storage.
  • We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to third parties.

4. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can update your profile details (username, avatar, native language) at any time in your account settings.
  • You have the right to log out or request the complete deletion of your account along with all learning data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or requests regarding the processing of your personal data and privacy, please contact us at:
