LingoIQ Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how LingoIQ uses cookies, local storage (localStorage), and similar technologies to ensure smooth and reliable functionality across our website.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

  • Cookies: Small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit its pages.
  • Local Storage (localStorage): A modern web browser feature that enables storing interface settings and data directly in your browser without transmitting them back to the server with every request.

2. What Technologies Do We Use and Why?

LingoIQ relies strictly on strictly necessary technical cookies and localStorage parameters:

  • Authentication & Security (Firebase Authentication): We use session tokens to keep you logged in securely. This prevents you from having to re-enter your password on every page reload or navigation step.
  • Interface Preferences: We store your chosen interface language, active learning stages, and visual settings in your browser's localStorage for a seamless user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize Google Firebase infrastructure for user authentication and database management. Google Firebase may set technical cookies required for security and verification purposes.

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies for targeted marketing or ad analytics.

4. How to Control Cookies

You can adjust your browser settings at any time to block or delete cookies and clear localStorage.

Please note: If you block essential cookies or clear localStorage, you will be logged out of LingoIQ, and your local interface preferences will be reset.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as necessary. Any updates will be published directly on this page.
